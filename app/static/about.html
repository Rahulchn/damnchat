Our story

Founded in January 2026, Damn Chat is built around a simple idea: the best part of the internet is the people you spend time with. We focus on straightforward tools for conversation, sharing, and watching together.

We continue to improve the product in small, practical steps. You can follow its development in our public repository.

Transparency before promises

Damn Chat does not currently provide verified identities, end-to-end encryption, or automated moderation. The public lounge is public, and anyone holding a room link can access its history and images. Please don't share sensitive information.

Media playback depends on the original provider's permissions and availability. We do not bypass sign-in requirements, age checks, or embedding restrictions.

Help & support

Found a bug or have a product suggestion? Open a GitHub issue ↗. This is a public support channel, not a private help desk.

Don't include private room links, credentials, personal information, or unredacted chat screenshots in an issue. There is no in-app moderation or guaranteed support response time yet.
